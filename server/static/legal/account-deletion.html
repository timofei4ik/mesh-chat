Delete your account

This permanently deletes the account and associated server data. It cannot be undone.

What is removed

Profile data, devices, stories, stored messages and media owned only by the account, subscriptions and owned groups/channels are removed under the service deletion policy. Limited security or moderation records may be retained where required for safety or law.

Delete in the app

You can also use Settings → Security → Delete account. The same server-side deletion process is used.
