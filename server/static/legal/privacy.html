Privacy Policy

How MeshChat handles account, communication, device and diagnostic data.

Effective: 18 August 2026

Data we process

  • Account data: login, verified email, password hash, profile name, username, avatar, description and device identifiers.
  • User content: messages, comments, stories, stickers, files, photos, video and voice messages needed to deliver and synchronize the service.
  • Optional data: location only when you explicitly share it, nearby Bluetooth data while Nearby mode is active, and microphone/camera/photo access when you use those features.
  • Technical data: IP address, connection and security logs, crash reports, performance diagnostics, app version and device platform.
  • Subscription data: entitlement state and transaction references. Payment card details are handled by the payment platform, not MeshChat.

How data is used

We use data to authenticate accounts, deliver and synchronize communications, prevent abuse, operate calls and media, provide support, enforce community rules, diagnose failures and maintain subscriptions. AI features send only the content selected for that feature to the configured AI provider; do not submit sensitive content you do not want processed for that request.

Encryption and safety

MeshChat encrypts supported text and media before delivery. Metadata required for routing, synchronization, abuse prevention and account operation is processed by the server. No system can guarantee absolute security, so keep your password and recovery access private.

Retention and deletion

Content is retained while needed to provide synchronization and until it is deleted under the product rules. Security logs and moderation records may be retained for a limited period to prevent abuse or meet legal duties. You may permanently delete your account in Settings → Security or through the web deletion page.

Third parties

MeshChat uses infrastructure providers and may use Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Performance, push notification services, payment providers, map tiles and AI providers when those functions are enabled. Their processing is limited to operating the selected feature.

Your choices

You can control online status, avatar, description, calls, group invites, notifications, location sharing and Bluetooth access. You can report content, block users, delete messages where available, export saved files and delete your account.

Children

MeshChat is not directed to children under the minimum digital-consent age applicable in their country. Users must provide accurate age information where requested and follow local law.

Contact

Use the public support form for privacy, safety or account requests.
