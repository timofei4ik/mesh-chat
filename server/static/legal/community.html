Community Guidelines

MeshChat is for private conversation and healthy communities, not abuse.

Do not post or facilitate

  • Child sexual abuse or exploitation, grooming, trafficking or non-consensual intimate material.
  • Credible threats, incitement, glorification of violence, terrorism or instructions intended to cause harm.
  • Harassment, stalking, bullying, doxxing, hateful attacks or discrimination.
  • Fraud, impersonation, phishing, spam, malware or deceptive financial activity.
  • Illegal goods, services or instructions, or content that violates another person's rights.

Reports and blocking

Long-press a message to report it. Profiles and group/channel information pages also provide clearly labelled report actions. Blocking prevents further interaction from that user on your device. Reports enter the moderation queue and are reviewed according to risk and available context.

Enforcement

Possible actions include keeping content, hiding or removing it, warnings, feature restrictions, account suspension or permanent blocking. Severe child-safety or imminent-threat reports are prioritized. Repeated misuse of reporting tools may also be restricted.

Appeals and contact

Use Support and include the report or account details needed to review the decision.
